1. KEYSTORE GENERATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHY: Signs your app so Google Play can verify it's really you

DO THIS ONCE ONLY:
    Location: D:\NEW_PROJECTS\Habitera\android\
    Command: keytool -genkey -v -keystore habitera_release.jks `
             -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 `
             -alias habitera_release_key

WHEN ASKED, ENTER:
    What is your first and last name? 
    ➜ Your Name
    
    What is your organizational unit?
    ➜ Engineering / Development
    
    What is your organization?
    ➜ Your Company Name
    
    What is your City or Locality?
    ➜ Your City
    
    What is your State or Province?
    ➜ Your State
    
    What is the two-letter country code for this unit?
    ➜ US (or your country code)
    
    Is CN=Your Name, OU=Engineering, ... correct?
    ➜ yes

RESULT:
    ✓ Creates file: habitera_release.jks
    ✓ File location: D:\NEW_PROJECTS\Habitera\android\
    ✓ File size: ~2-3 KB
    ✓ Valid for: 10,000 days (~27 years)


2. CONFIGURE SIGNING CREDENTIALS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FILE: D:\NEW_PROJECTS\Habitera\android\key.properties

CURRENT (TEMPLATE):
    storePassword=YOUR_STORE_PASSWORD
    keyPassword=YOUR_KEY_PASSWORD
    keyAlias=habitera_release_key
    storeFile=habitera_release.jks

CHANGE TO (with YOUR passwords):
    storePassword=THE_PASSWORD_YOU_ENTERED_IN_KEYSTORE_STEP
    keyPassword=THE_PASSWORD_YOU_ENTERED_IN_KEYSTORE_STEP
    keyAlias=habitera_release_key
    storeFile=habitera_release.jks

⚠️ IMPORTANT:
    • storePassword and keyPassword are what YOU entered
    • If you entered same password twice, use it for both
    • Do NOT add quotes
    • Do NOT add spaces


3. UPDATE APP PACKAGE NAME
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT: Change from default "com.example.habitera" to YOUR unique package name

CHOOSE YOUR PACKAGE NAME:
    Examples:
    • com.mycompany.habitera
    • com.myname.habitera
    • io.github.username.habitera
    • com.mydomain.habitera
    
    My choice: ___________________________________

RULES:
    ✓ Lowercase letters only
    ✓ Use dots to separate parts
    ✓ Must be UNIQUE
    ✗ No uppercase letters
    ✗ No spaces
    ✗ No leading numbers
    ✗ No special characters

⚠️  CANNOT BE CHANGED AFTER FIRST RELEASE!

UPDATE FILE 1: android/app/build.gradle.kts
    Find line ~25:
        applicationId = "com.example.habitera"
    
    Change to:
        applicationId = "com.yourcompany.habitera"

UPDATE FILE 2: android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
    Find line 1:
        <manifest xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android">
    
    Change to:
        <manifest xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android"
            package="com.yourcompany.habitera">

⚠️  USE SAME PACKAGE NAME IN BOTH FILES!
